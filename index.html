Welcome to Official Website

ESDP works for educational development, skill-based certification and vocational training support. The program focuses on practical learning, online record management and transparent verification systems.

Our purpose is to provide accessible training support for learners from urban and rural areas through structured courses, transparent records and centre-based guidance. ESDP is committed to quality education and skill development.
